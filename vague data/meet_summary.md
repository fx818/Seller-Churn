Package Types and Initial Onboarding
New clients are typically offered MDC (basic package) first, with the option to upsell to Star Leader after 1-2 months of usage
NHD vertical teams are restricted from selling Star Leader packages to new clients and must refer such requests to the service team
NHD vertical is limited to selling maximum TSI packages

Payment Modes and Requirements
Current payment mix is approximately 60-70% annual and 30% monthly
Annual packages require full upfront payment, while monthly packages work on NAJ (EMI) basis with automatic deductions from current accounts
Current account is mandatory for monthly packages — savings or salary accounts are not accepted for onboarding
Current accounts are typically opened under company/firm names, not individual names
Suppliers without current accounts must purchase annual packages

Churn Patterns and Statistics
First month (30-day) churn: Approximately 10% of clients do not complete their first EMI payment
Subsequent months: EMI cut rates decline from 90% (first month) to 80% (second month) to 70% (third month)
After 3 months, approximately 30% of suppliers have churned
Lock-in period is mentioned as 6 months but is not enforceable through written agreements
Clients can stop payments after 31 days without refund (money for first month is retained), but further cancellation requires going through retention process

Primary Churn Reasons
Main factors identified:

Less buy leads — insufficient lead volume
Non-genuine buy leads — buyers not responding or not serious
Sellers not providing time — suppliers too busy with local business to manage platform
Monthly clients feel less pressure to engage; annual clients more committed due to upfront payment

Other contributing factors:

Unrealistic expectations set during sales process
Poor quality expectations — expecting immediate leads and sales
Impatient clients who expect results within days
Inability to convert leads into sales
Pre-hosting cancellations due to GST issues, missing documentation, or personal emergencies

Retention Process
When clients request to cancel, retention team (service team with L1, L2, L3, L4 layers) works to resolve objections and retain them
Approximately 40-50% of cancellation requests are successfully retained
Social media complaints receive exception handling with expedited resolution
Process typically takes 2-3 months of retention efforts before final churn

Winback Strategy
Cool-off periods enforced:
Monthly churned clients: 6 months cool-off before re-onboarding is allowed
Annual churned clients: 3 months cool-off

After cool-off, clients can only return on annual packages, not monthly
Winback team assigned after cool-off period to pitch annual packages
Winback pitch focuses on:
Platform improvements (buy leads now go to 3-4 suppliers instead of 10)
Enriched buy lead information (50+ data points vs. 3 basic fields from 10 years ago)
Profile enrichment and catalog updates
Commitment to provide time for platform engagement


High Churn Categories and Geographic Restrictions
High churn categories identified through historical data analysis include apparel/textiles
Categories flagged as high churn are restricted to annual-only sales
Geographic restrictions applied to cities with poor historical performance:
Lucknow, Kanpur, Saharanpur, Surat, Jaipur restricted to annual-only sales
Tirupur branch closed due to high churn in textile market

High churn scoring based on multiple factors:
Quick churn patterns
Not picking up buyer calls
Not replying to buyer inquiries
Low buy lead consumption
Not giving time to platform


Sales Checklist and Restrictions
15-parameter sales checklist runs automatically on backend to determine if sales are allowed
Checklist determines: "Sale Not Allowed", "Monthly Allowed", or "Only Annual Allowed"
Negative categories (e.g., drugs, harmful products) are completely restricted and not allowed on platform
Sales attempted on restricted suppliers are automatically cancelled by production team

Revenue Impact and Refunds
Monthly-to-annual restrictions initially impact sales for 1-2 months but recover by month 3
Annual packages generate better revenue and higher sales incentives
Approximately 10-20% refund rate on payments received
Refunds only provided in exceptional circumstances (e.g., GST cancellation, personal emergencies)

Platform Improvements
Buy leads now limited to 3-4 suppliers instead of previous 10, increasing conversion opportunity
Consumed buy leads are now hidden from other suppliers
Buy lead information enriched from 3 basic fields to 50+ data points including buyer details, timing, contact methods, and more
